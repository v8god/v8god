## Introduction

I'm **Pratham Goyal** — an AI Engineer, Computer Vision Researcher, and Intelligent Systems Developer, currently pursuing a **B.Tech in Computer Science & Engineering at Amity University, Noida** (expected graduation June 2028).

My work lives at the intersection of perception and interaction — systems that can *see*, *reason*, and *respond* the way a person would. That means computer vision pipelines, multimodal AI agents, memory-driven assistants, and desktop software that feels less like a tool and more like a collaborator.
